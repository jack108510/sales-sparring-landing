Neil Rackham

SPIN Framework

Master the four-question sequence — Situation, Problem, Implication, Need-payoff — that surfaces real buying intent.

Challenger Reframe

Deliver a commercial insight that reframes how the prospect sees their problem and creates urgency to act.

Sandler Reversing

Turn pressure around. Use strategic reverses to keep the prospect talking and maintain equal business stature.
