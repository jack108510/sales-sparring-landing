How it works

From download to your first scored call in under two minutes.

1

Pick a scenario

Choose from 20+ realistic buyers across industries — each with their own pain points, personality, and call objectives. Or start with a random call.

2

Make the call

The AI buyer picks up. Talk naturally — it listens, responds, and pushes back just like a real prospect. Track your talk ratio and hit live checkpoints in real time.

3

Get your scorecard

Receive an instant grade (A–F) with detailed breakdowns across six selling skills, specific feedback on what worked, and what to say next time.

4

Track your progress

Every call is saved to your Call Library. Watch your scores climb, beat your personal bests, and build daily practice streaks that compound.
