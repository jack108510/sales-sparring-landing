Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 29, 2026

Sales Sparring ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the Sales Sparring mobile application and website.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: Email address and password when you create an account.
  • Usage data: Call duration, scores, and practice session history to power your analytics dashboard.
  • Voice data: Audio from your practice calls is processed in real time through our AI voice provider (Retell AI) to enable the AI buyer conversation. We do not store raw audio recordings.
  • Payment information: Subscription payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store credit card numbers — Stripe handles all payment data in accordance with PCI-DSS standards.
  • Device information: Basic device and OS information for app compatibility purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the Sales Sparring app and its features
  • To generate your call scores and analytics using AI analysis (OpenAI)
  • To manage your subscription and enforce plan limits
  • To send important service updates (no marketing emails without consent)
  • To improve the app and fix bugs

3. Voice & AI Processing

Practice calls use Retell AI to power the AI buyer voice. Call transcripts are processed by OpenAI to generate your scorecard and feedback. These transcripts are used solely for scoring purposes and are not used to train AI models. We do not sell or share your conversation data with third parties for advertising.

4. Microphone Access

The app requires microphone access to conduct practice calls. Microphone access is only active during a live practice call. You can revoke microphone permission at any time in your device Settings, though this will prevent practice calls from functioning.

5. Data Storage

Your account data, call history, and scores are stored securely in Supabase (PostgreSQL), hosted on servers in the United States. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with service providers necessary to operate the app:

  • Retell AI — voice call processing
  • OpenAI — call scoring and feedback generation
  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Supabase — database and authentication

7. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Call history is stored indefinitely unless you delete it through the app's Settings page. You may request complete account deletion by contacting us.

8. Children's Privacy

Sales Sparring is intended for users 17 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it promptly.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Data portability

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at jack@wildroseautomations.ca.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via email or in-app notification. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
